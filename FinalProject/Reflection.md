A) Concept & Intent 

We wanted to create a system where thcre user interacts with a pressure sensor to control plants in a virtual garden. Creating a microcosm of reality, enabling the user to experience the circle of life in a very fast time frame without actually affecting the world around them. 

B) Interaction Logic 

As the user exerts different level of pressure on the sensor, different species of flowers grow. As the space becomes more crowded, the older flowers die. Mimicking the fight for resources in the natural world. Utilising an arduino connected to a pressure sensor, mapping the output throuh P5.js.

D) Behavior Over Time 

As the user presses on the sensor, a random flower starts growing. The level of pressure exerted changes how big the flower initially starts. Additinally as time passes and the scene gets busier, the older flowers begin to wither and wilt. Until they dissapear. This process imitates reality. 

E) Aesthetic Decisions 

We chose a dark background to contrast and emphasise the vivid flowers, influenced by the cyber botanical movement. We chose a magically chime noise for when the flower appears to emphasise the futuristic generation of the flowers. This also ensured that the appearence of new flowers was emphasised and more obvious. Especially if the user is visually impaired or prefers audio cues. 

F) Iteration & Challenges 

As each of member of our team worked on a different aspect, it was difficult trying to combine them effectively. It resulted in our program lagging as it was difficult to render the flower generation adn the three dimensional appearence smoothly together. Making the overall experience moderately frustrating. Therefore if we did this again, we should have combined our seperate aspects earlier on leaving more time for troubleshooting.
